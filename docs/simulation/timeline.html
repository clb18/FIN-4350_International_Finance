Simulation Timeline

1 Overview

The simulation is organized into three acts. You will experience concepts in the order they occur in the real world: stable conditions first, then shock and crisis response, then reconstruction.

Tip

Key idea: Early choices create constraints later. Consistency and credibility matter.

3 Week-by-week map

3.1 Prep (Weeks 1–2)

  • Course foundations; simulation mechanics; role training; practice exercise (non-graded)

3.2 Act I: Foundations and stability (Weeks 3–6)

  • Week 3: national priorities and exchange-rate regime
  • Week 4: balance of payments adjustment
  • Week 5: FX market pressure and parity spillovers
  • Week 6: intervention, credibility, and currency defense

3.3 Act II: Shock and realignment (Weeks 7–8)

  • Week 7: regional war shock; trade and capital disruptions; emergency policy response
  • Week 8: realignment; successor states formed; credibility reset

3.4 Act III: Reconstruction and new system (Weeks 9–12)

  • Week 9: firm-level exposure and domestic conflict
  • Week 10: credibility repair; capital access; funding choices
  • Week 11: tax competition and trade finance; coordination challenges
  • Week 12: new international system; final presentations
